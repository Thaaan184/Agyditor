# AGY Video Studio: Default Design & Brand Direction (Option A)

Reading this as: Multi-profile local open-source video studio combining WeftCut MCP timeline editing, OneTake continuous-take motion films, HyperFrames GSAP/Three.js compositions, and audio-first clipping/SFX skills, dial `ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 3`.

## 1. Anti-Slop Dials (Default Studio Baseline)
- **ENERGY: 2 (Balanced-to-Bold):** Clean visual hierarchy and purposeful typography without neon or rainbow clutter.
- **RHYTHM: 3 (Varied & Asymmetric):** Every beat varies in duration, framing, and density. Uniform slide-show cadence is rejected by OneTake's `probe.py` / `verify_promo.py`.
- **MOTION: 3 (Choreographed & Continuous):** Elements carry across beat boundaries using measured `studio_kit/motion_primitives.js`, OneTake springs (`lib/motion.js`), GSAP timelines (`hyperframes`), Three.js `r186` scenes, or WeftCut bezier keyframes and Motifs rather than generic fade-up template loops.

## 2. Core Palette, Tactile Stage & Mixed-Voice Typography Rules (One-Line Reasons per R-31)
- **Default Ground (`#F4EFE6` Tactile Craft Paper / `#1B3B2B` Cutting Mat / `#F8FAFC` Light Paper):** Anchors diagrams, title inserts, and typography in bright physical media rather than defaulting to generic dark-carbon neon grids (`#0B0F17` dark is used strictly when a brief asks for nocturnal mood).
- **Natural Footage + Authentic NLE Overlays:** Foreground real camera footage, real transcripts, and compact NLE waveform strips derived from actual audio buffers instead of floating wireframe orbs or abstract 3D blobs (`antislop` R-01/R-07).
- **Primary Text (`#18181B` Deep Ink on Paper / `#F8FAFC` Ivory Ink on Cutting Mat & Dark):** Guarantees >12:1 WCAG AAA contrast even during camera whips.
- **Muted Technical Metadata (`#52525B` on Light / `#A1A1AA` on Dark):** Separates NLE timestamps and small audio cue labels without competing against the main frame.
- **Single Deliberate Accent (`#D97706` Craft Amber / `#22C55E` NLE Waveform Green):** Reserved exclusively for active playheads, dip arrows, and active stereo waveform peaks (R-29).
- **Mandatory Bundled Fonts (`@font-face` + Mixed-Voice Typography):** Windows lacks `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter`, and `Montserrat` system-wide, so unbundled stacks fall back to `Segoe UI` and look like web dialogs. Every composition and Motif MUST load self-hosted fonts from `studio_kit/fonts/` via `@font-face` and await `document.fonts.load(...)`. Pair Mixed-Voice contrast cleanly: tight bold geometric sans (`Inter Tight` 800, `-0.04em` tracking) + italic editorial serif (`Instrument Serif` / `Fraunces` italic, normalized via `font-size-adjust: from-font` with `@supports not` fallback) + tabular monospace (`JetBrains Mono` / `Geist Mono`) strictly for telemetry (R-06).

## 5. Sound Philosophy, 5-Stem Separation & Zero-Waste Storytelling
- **Show First, Name Second & Causal Proof:** Lead with concrete visual movement (`real footage`, tactile paper cards, or live NLE waveform dips) before naming concepts; abstract 3D placeholders (`wireframe prism`, `octahedron`, glowing blobs) are rejected by `beat_sheet.py`.
- **No Empty Digital Vacuums:** Anchor quiet scenes with a filtered local room-tone bed (`ambience`, `-36` to `-40 dBFS`) except inside scheduled `silence_pockets`, where music and SFX cut cleanly (`-30` to `-55 dB` contrast) for A/B proof or comedic timing.
- **Every Cue Needs a Physical Visual Cause:** Stack `sub_low` (`<= 155 Hz`), `mid_body` (`260-2800 Hz`), and `high_air` (`>= 2600 Hz`) layers (`50-240 ms` J-cut lead) only where `visual_cause` on screen motivates the sound (or an explicit `contrast_rule` specifies intentional absence/comedy).
- **Coherent Style Locking & Emotional Realism:** Lock SFX choices to a single palette in `studio_kit/sound_palette_index.json` unless an explicit `Emotional Realism` narrative note maps tactile organic textures onto literal shots.
- **Music Carving & Great Hall Tails:** Carve speech frequencies out of active music tracks via adaptive spectral pockets, ease into loops with reversed beat risers, and resolve endings through a `Great Hall` convolution tail (`T60 = 2.6s`).
- **Pre/Post Profile-Aware Gates:** Validate `studio_kit/beat_sheet.py` before pixel rendering and pass `studio_kit/acoustic_audit.py` after encoding (`-18.5..-13.0 LUFS`, Peak `<= -3.5 dBFS`, using `narrative_voice_film` limits `mud <= 48%` / `voice >= 18%` for warm dialogue films and `mograph_sfx` limits `mud < 35%` / `detail+air >= 4.0%` for motion SFX spots).
