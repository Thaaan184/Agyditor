# NCT AI Motion Graphic: Design Direction & Anti-Slop Justification

Reading this as: 12-second continuous-take enterprise infrastructure motion film for PT Nalar Cerdas Teknologi (`pt-nct.ai`), in an architectural telemetry visual language, dial `ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 3`.

## 1. Anti-Slop Dials
- **ENERGY: 2 (Balanced Precision):** High-contrast slate-and-sky engineering telemetry matching `pt-nct.ai`'s actual enterprise identity (`#0F172A`, `#F8FAFC`, `#0EA5E9`, `#10B981`).
- **RHYTHM: 3 (Asymmetric 4-Beat Progression):**
  - Beat 1 (`0.0s` to `3.0s`, 3.0s duration): Single horizontal latency timeline (`22.0s` conventional waste compressing to `<0.8s` HAD).
  - Beat 2 (`3.0s` to `6.2s`, 3.2s duration): 3-way vertical BGP carrier routing tree (`Telkomsel 21ms`, `XL Axiata 24ms`, `Indosat 26ms`).
  - Beat 3 (`6.2s` to `9.4s`, 3.2s duration): Asymmetric live CRM dispatch wallboard (`1,240 / 2,000 Channels`, `482 Voicemails Filtered`, `11 Modul CRM`).
  - Beat 4 (`9.4s` to `12.0s`, 2.6s duration): Centered architectural lockup with specific consultation CTA (`pt-nct.ai/contact`).
- **MOTION: 3 (Continuous Physical Carry):** Driven deterministically by `window.__seek(t)`. At every beat boundary, the central telemetry spine morphs physically into the next structure (Compression Bar -> 3 Carrier Fiber Trunks -> CRM Dispatch Grid -> NCT.ai Brand Lockup).

## 2. One-Line Purpose Justifications (`antislop` R-31)
- **Deep Slate Canvas (`#0F172A`) with Crisp Alabaster Text (`#F8FAFC`):** Replicates `pt-nct.ai`'s primary enterprise dark surface (`rgb(15, 23, 42)`) while achieving 15.4:1 WCAG AAA contrast for fast-moving telemetry numbers.
- **Telemetry Sky Accent (`#0EA5E9`) & Carrier Active Green (`#10B981`):** Distinguishes active `<0.8s` Human Answer Detection pulses (`#0EA5E9`) and verified `<26ms` live carrier interconnects (`#10B981`) from muted `22.0s` legacy voicemail waste (`#64748B`).
- **Geometric Display Sans + Tabular Monospace (`Inter` / `Segoe UI` + `JetBrains Mono` / `Consolas`):** Matches `pt-nct.ai`'s live CSS font stack while preventing horizontal jitter during frame-by-frame millisecond and channel count interpolation.
- **Zero Decorative Icons or Gradients:** Every line on screen represents either a time duration (`22.0s` vs `0.8s`), a physical fiber route (`JKT-IX -> Tier-1 Carrier`), or a live CRM channel counter.
